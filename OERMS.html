OEMS

The department of Office Equipment and Maintenanace services unit is under the E-GOVERNMENT Division - SMART Zambia Institute. the department was established in the late 1940's and its main objectives are enrished in the statutory Instrument No.122 of 1985 on control and management of public stores regulations.
The Unit is mandated to coordinate the acquisition, registration, maintenance, usage and disposal of Government office equipment in order to ensure adherence to set standards as outlined by SI. No. 77 of 2022 of the Public Finance Management (Public Stores) Regulations.


The following are the functions of the unit:

MAINTENANACE OF OFFICE EQUIPMENT

Maintenance of government office equipment in order to ensure serviceability and prolonged life span;
Maintain a record of repaired office equipment in order to ensure efficient decision making;

Standard Operation Procedure SOP

TECHINICAL GUIDANCE

Provides technical advice to Government institutions on the management of office equipment in order to promote standardization and easy maintenance;

OEMS Standard

DIPOSAL AND INSPECTION OF OFFICE EQUIPMENT

Inspection of Government office equipment in order to ensure adherence to set standards; and Identification of obsolete and unserviceable office equipment to facilitate disposal

ASSET INVENTORY

Assignment of range of Government serial numbers to Public Bodies for inscription on equipment in order to ensure accountability Maintain an inventory of all government office equipment in order to ensure accountability;

inscription Guidelines
